Manual Trade

Shell equivalent: buy, sell

This bypasses the engine's risk gate

  • The per-bot allocation, the orders-per-bar limit, the duplicate check and the kill switch do not apply.
  • The broker still checks price, quantity, trading limit, balance and inventory, and refuses with a reason.
  • A running bot will not know this happened: its ledger mirror is built from the engine's reports, so a manual fill moves the balance underneath it.

Always a limit ROD order on TSE — that is the only shape this broker path sends. No tick-size or lot-size rounding is applied; the price goes through as typed.
